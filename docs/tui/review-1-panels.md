# Review 1: detail panel and side panel

**Approve with changes.** No P0: every keyboard and mouse path that I tried works, and focus stays put through toggles and folds. 15 P1 and 21 P2 findings.
The largest problems are layout shift (the path wraps when the scrollbar shows, and lazy rows change the card height), hierarchy (bright descriptions, high risk hidden below the fold), and charts that do not tell the truth at 40 cells.

Evidence: `out/review/panels/*.png` (crops in `out/review/panels/crop/`), from `out/review/shots.py`, `out/review/pilot_paths.py`, and `out/review/pilot_states.py`. All use DemoAtlas.
